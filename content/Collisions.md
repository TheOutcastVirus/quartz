- Different types of collisions have different conservations

| Type of Collision | Is [[Momentum]] Conserved? | Is Kinetic [[Energy]] Conserved? |
| ----------------- | -------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Elastic           | Yes                        | Yes                              |
| Inelastic         | Yes                        | No                               |
- A perfectly inelastic collision means they stick together completely
- A collision involving a hard sphere are nearly elastic 
- Most collisions are inelastic
	- This means that some energy is dissipated during the collision due to heat, sound created during the collision
