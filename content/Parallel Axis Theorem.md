We can use the parallel axis theorem to calculate the [[Moment of Inertia|moment of inertia]] for a an object about some axis if we know the moment of inertia about its center of mass. 
$$
I = I_{cm} +mD^{2}
$$
Conditions:
- Constant density
- $m$: total mass
- $D$: Distance from center of mass to the new axis of rotation.
