- Power is the change in [[work]] over time
- Average power can be defined as 
	- $$
	P_{\text{avg}} = \frac{W}{\Delta t}
	$$
- And the instantaneous power can be defined as 
	- $$
	P_{\text{inst}} = \frac{dW}{dt}
	$$
	- Which can be rewritten as 
	- $$
	P = \frac{d}{dt}(\vec{F} \cdot \Delta \vec{r})
	$$
- For a constant force, the power can be described as 
	- $$
	P = \vec{F} \cdot \frac{d\Delta \vec{r}}{dt}
$$
	- $$
	P = \vec{F} \cdot \vec{v}
$$
