$$
-F_{s} = ma_{x} \implies-kx = ma_{x} \implies a_{x} =\frac{k}{m}x
$$
Therefore, the maximum acceleration is equal to:
$$
a_{max} = \frac{k}{m}A
$$
Where $A$ is the maximum distance from equilibrium position.

Recall that the condition for [[simple harmonic motion]]:

$$
\frac{d^{2}x}{dt^{2}} = -\omega^{2} x
$$
Substitute the acceleration of a spring with the acceleration:
$$
\frac{k}{m}x = -\omega^{2} x \implies \omega = \sqrt{\frac{k}{m}}
$$
Which is used in the equations for simple harmonic equation. 

The period of a spring is derived by:
$$
\omega = \sqrt{ \frac{k}{m} } = \frac{2\pi}{T}
$$
$$
T = 2\pi \sqrt{ \frac{m}{k} }
$$
