- When an object is in uniform circular motion ($\alpha  = 0$), the only translation acceleration it has is towards the center
	- This is the centripetal acceleration

$$
a_{c} = \frac{v_{t}^{2}}{r} = r \omega^{2}
$$
- If a object is accelerating, it has centripetal acceleration plus [[Tangential Velocity and Acceleration|tangential acceleration]]
