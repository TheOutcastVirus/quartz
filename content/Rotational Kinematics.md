- Rotational speed is the derivative of angular position with regards to time
	- $$
	\omega_{avg} = \frac{\Delta \theta}{\Delta t}
	$$
	- $$
	\omega_{inst} = \frac{d\theta}{dt} 
	$$
- Rotational acceleration is the derivative of rotational speed with regards to time 
	- $$
	\alpha_{avg} = \frac{\Delta\omega}{\Delta t} 
	$$
	- $$
	\alpha_{inst} = \frac{d\omega}{dt}
	$$
- All of the kinematics equations are the same for rotational and translation [[kinematics]], but with different variables
