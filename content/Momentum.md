- Momentum is a vector that is defined as:

$$
\vec{p} = m \vec{v} \implies \frac{kg\cdot m}{s}
$$
- [[Newton's Laws|Newton's second law]] can be written to relate to momentum as:

$$
\Sigma \vec{F}  = \frac{d\vec{p}}{dt} = \frac{d}{dt} (m\vec{v}) = \frac{dm}{dt}\vec{v}+m\frac{d\vec{v}}{dt}
$$
	- Assuming that the mass of the remains constant ($\frac{dm}{dt} = 0$), we get the original form of newton's first law

$$
\Sigma \vec{F} = m \vec{a}
$$
- Conservation of momentum holds when all the forces are internal to the system

$$
\Sigma \vec{F}_{\text{external}} = 0 = \frac{d\vec{p}}{dt} \implies \Sigma \vec{p}_{i} = \Sigma \vec{p}_{f}
$$
