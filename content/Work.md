- Work is the change in [[energy]] caused by a force along some distance 
- Work is defined for a constant force as:

$$
W = \vec{F} \cdot \Delta \vec{r} = F\Delta r\cos \theta
$$
	- Work is a defined as a scalar
- The unit for work is the Joule $J =  N \cdot m$
- Work by a non-constant force is

$$
W = \int_{x_{i}}^{x_{f}} F_{x}dx
$$
- By the same theorem, we can derive that kinetic energy is equal to 

$$
KE = \frac{1}{2}m^{2}
$$
