The angular frequency for a pendulum with length $L$ and gravity of $g$ is:
$$
\omega = \sqrt{ \frac{g}{L} }
$$
Therefore, the period of a pendulum is:
$$
T = 2\pi \sqrt{ \frac{L}{g} }
$$
For a physical pendulum, the period is equal to:
$$
T_{phys} = \sqrt{ \frac{I}{mgd} }
$$
