- The force of gravity in a constant gravitation field is 
	- $$
F_{g} = m_{o}g
	$$
	- $g = 9.81 m/s^2$ 
- Newton's law of universal gravitation is:
	- $$
	F_{g} = \frac{Gm_{1}m_{2}}{r^{2}}
	$$
	- $r$ is the distance from the center of masses of the two objects
