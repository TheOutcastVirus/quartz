Torque is a form of rotation that acts on rotating bodies. Torque is a vector quantity that points away from the plane of rotation.

The magnitude of torque can be defined as:
$$
\tau = rF\sin \theta
$$
Torque can be defined as a vector by the cross  product:
$$
\vec{\tau} = \vec{r} \times \vec{F}
$$
An object is in rotational equilibrium is the next torque acting on it is equal to zero. 
$$
\sum \vec{\tau} = 0
$$
> If an object is in translational equilibrium and in rotational equilibrium about one axis of rotation, then the object is in rotational equilibrium about any axis of rotation.
