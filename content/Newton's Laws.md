- Dynamics includes Newton's laws and friction

## Newton's 1st Law
- when viewed from an inertial reference frame, an object in motion will remain in motion unless acted upon
	- Called the law on inertia
## Newton's  2nd Law
$$
\Sigma \vec{F} = m\vec{a}
$$
## Newton's 3rd Law
$$
\vec{F}_{a} = -\vec{F}_{b}
$$
