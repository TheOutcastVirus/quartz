- Typically referred to as $F_{R}, R, F_{D}$
- Opposed the motion of an object
- The key equation is:

$$
\vec{F}_{R} = -b \vec{v}
$$
- The typical drag equation is:

$$
\vec{F}_{R} = \frac{1}{2}D \rho A v^2
$$
	- Where $D$ is the drag coefficient, $\rho$ is the density of medium, $A$ is the cross sectional area, and $v$ is the velocity of the object
	- This equation usually does not show up on the AP test however, and is usually given
- Terminal velocity is when the resistive force is equal to the force of gravity

$$
\Sigma \vec{F}_{y} = 0 = F_{R} - F_{g}
$$
	- Plug in the equation for the resistive force, and solve for velocity
