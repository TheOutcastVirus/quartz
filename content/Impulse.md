- Impulse is defined as the change in [[momentum]]

$$
\vec{J} = \int_{t_{i}}^{t_{f}} \Sigma \vec{F} dt = \Delta \vec{p}
$$
	- Note that this is the integral of force with respect to time. The integral with respect to position is the [[work]]
- The impulse approximation is when a large impact force can be assumed to be the only force acting on a system

$$
\Sigma \vec{F}  = \vec{F}_{im\frac{ \partial c }{ \partial t }} \implies \vec{F}_{impact} 
$$
- The impulse is also equal to the average force times the change in time

$$
\vec{J} = \vec{F}_{\text{avg} } \Delta t
$$
