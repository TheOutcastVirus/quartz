- The spring force is defined as:

$$
\vec{F}_{s} = -k \Delta \vec{x}
$$

	- where $k$ is the spring constant, $\Delta \vec{x}$ is the displacement from the spring's equilibrium position
		- The negative sign means that the spring force always is oppositive from the displacement
	- Units for $k$ are $\frac{N}{m}$
- The [[work]] done by a spring can be derived as:

$$
W_{F_{s}} = \int_{x_i}^{x_{f}} F_{s} ds =  \int_{x_i}^{x_{f}} (-kx) dx = \left( -\frac{kx^2}{2} \right) ]_{x_{i}}^{x_{f}}
$$
	- Note that this equation is similar to the equation for [[work|kinetic energy]]
- Objects on springs can be in [[Simple Harmonic Motion]]
