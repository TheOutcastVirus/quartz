- The equations for uniformly accelerated motion ($a$ is a constant) are
	- $$
	v_{x} = v_{x_{0}} a_{x}t
	$$
	- $$
	x = x_0 + v_{x_{0}}t + \frac{1}{2} a_{x}t^2
	$$
	- $$
	v_{x}^2 = v_{x_{0}}^2 + 2a_{x} (x-x_{0})
	$$
- Under free fall, the only acceleration acting on an object is [[gravity]], which is a case of UAM (g is constant near the surface of the earth)
