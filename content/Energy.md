- For an non-isolated system, the total change in the energy is the sum of the transfers of energy

$$
\Delta E=\Sigma T
$$
- For an isolated system, the total change in energy is equal to zero.

$$
\Delta E = 0
$$
- Therefore, the sum of the change of mechanical energy and change of internal energy must be 0. 

$$
\Delta ME + \Delta E_{\text{internal}} = 0
$$
	- Where $\Delta E_{\text{internal}} = -W_{NC}$, the work done by [[Conservative and Non-conservative Forces|non-conservative]] forces, (i.e. [[Friction]])
- When using equations for the conservation of energy, identify the:
	- Initial point
	- final point
	- horizontal zero line
