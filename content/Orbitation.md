- The binding energy is the energy required to remove two objects that are [[gravity|gravitationally]] attracted to each other

$$
W_{F_{a}} = -U_{gi} = \frac{Gm_{1}m_{2}}{r}
$$
	- The work is equal to the negative of the [[gravitational potential energy]]
- The escape velocity is velocity to leave the gravitational field completely 
	- derived by setting the change in kinetic energy equal to change in gravitational potential energy

$$
\Delta KE = \Delta U_{g}
$$
	- the velocity of the object in orbit is equal to the velocity obtained when the force of gravity is set to the force of gravity
