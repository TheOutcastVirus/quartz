- Arc length is equal to:
	- $$
	s = r \Delta \theta
	$$
- Derivative of this with regards with time is the tangential velocity
	- $$
	\frac{d}{dt}(s=r\Delta \theta) \implies \frac{ds}{dt}=r \frac{d(\Delta \theta)}{dt} = v_{t} = r \omega
	$$
- Similarly, the tangential acceleration is:
	- $$
	a_{t} = r \alpha
	$$
